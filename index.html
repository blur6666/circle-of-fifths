The Circle of Fifths

Rings, outside in — key signature on the treble staff (left column = the five lines F D B G E, right column = the four spaces E C A F; filled boxes are the notes that get a sharp or a flat), major key, relative minor, diminished chord of the key.
The top sector and the three at the bottom show two spellings of the same pitch, so they carry two key signatures: flat spelling on the left, sharp spelling on the right.
The mask fades everything but one key: three majors, their three relative minors, the three key signatures, and the one diminished chord in the middle. Use the arrows to step round the circle.
The accidental-count numbers are switched off for now — set SHOW_COUNTS = true at the top of script.js to bring them back.
