The Circle of Fifths

Outer ring — the key signature on the treble staff. Left column = the five lines (F D B G E), right column = the four spaces (E C A F). Filled boxes are the notes that get a sharp or a flat.
Middle ring — major key. Inner ring — its relative minor. The top sector and the three at the bottom show two spellings of the same pitch, so they carry two key signatures: flat spelling on the left, sharp spelling on the right.
The accidental-count numbers are switched off for now — set SHOW_COUNTS = true at the top of script.js to bring them back.
